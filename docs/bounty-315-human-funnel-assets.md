## Formatting / publishing notes

- Keep the first line of each post visible before any platform truncation.
- Use one CTA only; avoid stacking multiple links.
- Avoid guaranteed-earnings language, unsupported hardware performance claims, or implying every device qualifies.
- Prefer real photos of older hardware, original generated visuals, or rights-cleared images.
- For vertical video, use 9:16 framing and keep text inside the central safe area.
- Captions should be readable without audio.
- Test at least one nostalgia-first creative, one anti-upgrade creative, and one simple "check your old machine" creative before scaling.
