# wRTC Notice

> **The wRTC bridge is disabled.** RTC is earned for contributions and spent on services in the ecosystem; there is no off-ramp.

## How RTC works

RTC is **one way in, no way out**:

- **Earn it** — mining on real hardware, merged contributions, bounties, and other work for the ecosystem.
- **Buy it as credits** — on [BoTTube](https://bottube.ai), to spend on services.
- **Spend it** — on services inside the RustChain / BoTTube ecosystem.

There is no off-ramp, and the `$0.15` reference rate is an internal accounting unit, not a market price.

Read the full guide: **[Earn & Spend RTC](https://github.com/Scottcjn/rustchain-bounties/blob/main/docs/EARN_AND_SPEND.md)**.
